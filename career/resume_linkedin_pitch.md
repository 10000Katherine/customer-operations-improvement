## 90-second interview answer

“I wanted to demonstrate a complete approach to business process improvement, rather than build a dashboard without decisions attached. I started with a public synthetic dataset containing 4,000 SaaS support tickets. I defined operational KPIs, set a clearly disclosed assumed 72-hour resolution SLA, then built a Power BI report and validated key measures in SQLite.

The baseline showed 78.17% resolution SLA compliance and 824 breaches among resolved tickets. Technical accounted for 367 breaches and Integrations 171, together representing 65.3%. I also examined workload and priority distributions. The figures tell us where to investigate, but they do not establish why a ticket was delayed.

I proposed a 12-week DMAIC pilot focused on Technical Normal/Low cases, with clearer case ownership, an aging review, candidate 48-hour warnings and 60-hour escalations, while separately investigating Integrations handoffs. I defined weekly success measures and guardrails so that a perceived improvement cannot simply be achieved by neglecting high-priority work.

The goal is to demonstrate evidence-based business analysis, careful interpretation of data limits, and an actionable implementation plan. Since this is a simulation, I have not claimed any real operational improvement.”
